1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data submitted through this website (albertoperezgant.com) is collected and processed, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and applicable Spanish data protection law (LOPDGDD 3/2018).

2. What data is collected

The only personal data collected on this website is the information you voluntarily provide through the contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and does not track visitors.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Your data is processed for a single purpose: to read and reply to the message you send through the contact form.

The legal basis for this processing is your consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you give by ticking the acceptance checkbox before submitting the form. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to info@albertoperezgant.com.

4. Storage and retention

Form submissions are stored in a self-hosted database managed by the data controller. Your data will be kept only for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any follow-up communication, after which it will be deleted.

5. Recipients and transfers

Your data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and no international data transfers take place. No automated decision-making or profiling is performed.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, or for any claim related to the processing of your personal data, contact info@albertoperezgant.com. If you consider that your rights have not been adequately addressed, you may file a complaint with the Spanish supervisory authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or technical changes. The date of the latest revision is shown at the top of this page.
