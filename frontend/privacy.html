1. Scope

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you contact Alberto Perez Gant using the email link on this website. The site itself has no contact form, analytics, or advertising cookies.

2. What data is collected

The website does not collect information through a form. If you choose to contact Alberto by email, your email address, name (if included), and message content will be received:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

The email application and provider you use to send your message may process data under their own privacy terms.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Your message is processed to read and reply to your enquiry. The legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest in responding to messages and maintaining relevant correspondence (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

4. Storage and retention

Messages are handled in the controller's email account and kept only for as long as needed to respond and manage any related follow-up.

5. Recipients and transfers

Your message is handled by the email services involved in sending and receiving it. No automated decision-making or profiling is performed by the controller.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, or for any claim related to the processing of your personal data, contact info@albertoperezgant.com. If you consider that your rights have not been adequately addressed, you may file a complaint with the Spanish supervisory authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or technical changes. The date of the latest revision is shown at the top of this page.
